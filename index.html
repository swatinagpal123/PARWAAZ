Coorg

Coffee plantations, misty hills and peaceful surroundings.

Why Visit?

Perfect for slowing down and spending time away from the city.

Things to Do

Coffee plantations, Abbey Falls and Raja's Seat.

Best Time

October - March

Budget

₹2,500 - ₹5,000

Chikmagalur

Misty mountains, coffee estates and beautiful views.

Why Visit?

Perfect for nature lovers, peaceful trips and adventure.

Things to Do

Mullayanagiri, coffee estates and waterfalls.

Best Time

September - March

Budget

₹2,500 - ₹5,000

Sakleshpur

Green hills, coffee estates and quiet surroundings.

Why Visit?

A peaceful destination for anyone looking to escape the city.

Things to Do

Trekking, coffee plantations, waterfalls and nature walks.

Best Time

October - March

Budget

₹2,000 - ₹4,500

Nandi Hills

A quick escape from Bangalore with beautiful sunrise views.

Why Visit?

Popular for its sunrise, cool weather and beautiful views.

Things to Do

Watch the sunrise, explore the hills and visit Tipu's Drop.

Best Time

September - February

Budget

₹500 - ₹2,000
