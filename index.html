01 / COORG

Coorg

Coffee plantations, misty hills and peaceful surroundings make Coorg a perfect escape.

Why Visit?

Coorg is known for its coffee plantations, green landscapes and peaceful surroundings. It is a great place to slow down and spend some time away from the city.

Things to Do

Coffee plantations, Abbey Falls, Raja's Seat and Madikeri Fort.

Best Time

October – March

Budget

₹2,500 – ₹5,000

02 / CHIKMAGALUR

Chikmagalur

Misty mountains, coffee estates and beautiful views make Chikmagalur a favourite getaway.

Why Visit?

Chikmagalur is surrounded by coffee plantations and mountains. It is perfect for people who enjoy nature, peaceful places and adventure.

Things to Do

Mullayanagiri, coffee estates, waterfalls and trekking.

Best Time

September – March

Budget

₹2,500 – ₹5,000

03 / SAKLESHPUR

Sakleshpur

Green hills, coffee estates and quiet surroundings make Sakleshpur a relaxing escape.

Why Visit?

Sakleshpur is surrounded by forests, hills and coffee plantations. It is a peaceful destination for anyone looking to escape the city.

Things to Do

Trekking, coffee plantations, waterfalls and nature walks.

Best Time

October – March

Budget

₹2,000 – ₹4,500

04 / NANDI HILLS

Nandi Hills

A quick escape from Bangalore with beautiful sunrise views and peaceful mountain roads.

Why Visit?

Nandi Hills is one of the easiest escapes from Bangalore. It is especially popular for its sunrise, cool weather and beautiful views.

Things to Do

Watch the sunrise, explore the hills and visit Tipu's Drop.

Best Time

September – February

Budget

₹500 – ₹2,000
